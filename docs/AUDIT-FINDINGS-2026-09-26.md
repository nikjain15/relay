## 2. Regulatory verification

| Item | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **FINRA 2210 principal approval and retention** | Requirement CONFIRMED for retail communications, 2210(b)(1) and (b)(4). **But v0.1's headline claim was wrong.** Under 2210(a), **correspondence** is a written communication to **25 or fewer retail investors in any rolling 30 calendar-day period**, supervised under 3110(b) and 3110.06 through .09, with **no principal pre-approval required**. A personalised AI-drafted note to one household is correspondence. It becomes a retail communication at the 26th recipient in 30 days. Exceptions v0.1 glossed: communications making no recommendation and not promoting a product or service; institutional communications; previously filed templates |
| **Reg BI care obligation** | Substantially fair, imprecise. Term of art is "**reasonably available** alternatives," not all alternatives; **costs** are an explicit element; and **Reg BI does not itself mandate documenting** the alternatives analysis, that is the supervisory and examination expectation. Also Reg BI has **four** obligations (Disclosure, Care, Conflict of Interest, Compliance) and v0.1 cited only Care |
| **FINRA 3110** | **Correct.** 3110(a) supervisory system, 3110(b) written supervisory procedures |
| **SEC Rule 17a-4** | Citation correct, but "write-once" as **the** requirement is STALE. The **October 2022 amendments** added an **audit-trail alternative** permitting recreation of a modified or deleted record. WORM was retained as an option, not the requirement |
| **Advisers Act marketing rule** | **PARTIALLY CORRECT, and the brief's own flag overcorrected.** UBS Financial Services Inc. is a **dual registrant**, so 206(4)-1 governs advisory-capacity communications, FINRA 2210 governs brokerage, both apply firm-wide, and the stricter standard governs each artifact. Correctly framed this is a credential. Bonus current detail: FINRA's 2026 projections proposal does not fully align with the marketing rule's hypothetical-performance framework |
| **What the control map was missing** | Reg BI's other three obligations; FINRA 2111; FINRA 4511; SEC 17a-3(a)(35); Reg S-P as amended 2024; **FINRA Regulatory Notice 24-09 (June 2024)**, the on-point GenAI guidance, absent entirely; FINRA's 2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report GenAI section; third-party and vendor risk given Azure is a vendor; FINRA 3120 and 3130 annual testing and certification; and whether prompts and model outputs are themselves records. **Highest-value omission: SR 26-2**, the revised interagency model risk guidance issued 17 April 2026 by the Fed, OCC and FDIC, which replaced SR 11-7 **and expressly places generative and agentic AI outside its scope, expecting a separate governance framework** |

- **UBS Evidence Lab is real**, the alternative-data and primary-research arm of the Investment Bank,
  powering UBS Research and sold as a dataset subscription to institutional clients. Spun out of the
  research division as a standalone unit in 2018. **Sell-side, not wealth management.**

  strategy and then, at the level of the data model, substituted a publication-driven market-trigger
  graph for the alternative-data opportunity engine he built. The flagship reason path would have been
  visibly wrong to him. This was the worst finding in the audit.

  published ~90% adoption figure argues against its strong form. It must be a hypothesis, never a
  finding about their system.

  refusal precision and recall, reason paths from traversal). The weakness was never the vocabulary, it
  was that the vocabulary sat on top of the wrong signal model.

followed by a re-render and a gate check rather than done as a text edit alone.
