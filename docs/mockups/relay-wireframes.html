    Words used on these screens
    Opportunity
    Something UBS's AI flags about a client overnight, for example a large property sale.
    Cash cushion
    Months of planned spending held in cash-like investments. UBS's Wealth Way framework calls it Liquidity and sizes it at 2 to 5 years.
    Approved products
    The investments the firm allows advisors to recommend.
    Family's rules
    Limits agreed with the client, for example "keep at least two years of cash" or "no private credit". Formally, the investment policy statement.
    Required withdrawals
    Required minimum distributions from retirement accounts, which start at age 73 under SECURE 2.0.
    Escalated
    A form unsigned for more than 14 days goes to the branch supervisor, per the illustrative procedure.
    Callback
    Before money moves on an emailed instruction, the team calls the client on a number already on file. A standard control against wire fraud.
    Compliance check
    A supervising manager reviews what goes to clients before it is sent.
    The 25-person rule
    Under FINRA Rule 2210, a note that reaches more than 25 individual clients in 30 days needs a manager's approval before it is sent. Relay counts people, not families, across every advisor using the same note.
    Composite
    An invented person or family, built from situations UBS and public sources describe. Every source is listed in the Relay repo's PERSONAS.md.
