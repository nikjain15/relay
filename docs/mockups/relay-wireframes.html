Relay · low-fidelity mockup

One advisor, one client, six steps

UBS's AI flags more than 20 million client opportunities a year. Relay is what happens next: it helps an advisor turn one of them into an approved action for a client, with the reasons and the compliance sign-off on record. Follow one example, the Renner family, from start to finish. All names and numbers are made up.

    Three more screens, planned but not built

    Finding new clients

    Ranks people worth an introduction, for example someone with large savings at another firm. The advisor sends the introduction.

    Paperwork

    A checklist per client of forms still unsigned or due for renewal, with reminders drafted for the advisor.

    Service requests

    Sorts requests like "change my beneficiary" and routes each to the assistant who handles it.

    Words used on these screens
    Opportunity
    Something UBS's AI flags about a client, for example a large property sale.
    Approved products
    The list of investments the firm allows advisors to recommend.
    Family's rules
    Limits agreed with the client, for example "keep at least two years of cash" or "no private credit". Formally, the investment policy statement.
    Compliance check
    A manager at the firm reviews what goes to clients before it is sent.
    The 25-person rule
    Under FINRA Rule 2210, a note that reaches more than 25 individual clients in 30 days needs a manager's approval before it is sent. Relay counts people, not families, across every advisor using the same note.
    Synthetic
    Made up for the mockup. No real client or UBS data is used.
